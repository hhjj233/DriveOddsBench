# Third-party notices

This repository contains our code, the history lists in `data/` and the figures in `docs/assets/` (see below). Data, model code and weights are downloaded from their sources and keep their own licenses. The licenses below are those recorded by each kit when it pinned its sources (`generation/<model>/*_pins.json`); check the source before reuse.

## Data

- **Argoverse 2 Sensor dataset** (https://www.argoverse.org/av2.html): CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The frames shown in `docs/assets/` are Argoverse 2 frames or generated continuations of them.

## Instrument

- **torchvision** (BSD-3-Clause): the COCO Faster R-CNN v2 detector (`fasterrcnn_resnet50_fpn_v2`) and RAFT-large optical flow, with their released weights.
- **av2 devkit** (MIT): camera, pose and map access.
